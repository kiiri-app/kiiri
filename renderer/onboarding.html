How we get along

  • Drag me anywhere — I hang on while you carry me.
  • Click me and I'll perk up and listen.
  • Right-click for everything: pomodoro, reminders, AI, settings.

Give me a brain (optional)

With an AI model I can look at your screen, answer questions and make files. You can always set this up later.

Then try asking me:
  • Right-click ▸ Ask about my screen ▸ "What does this error mean?"
  • Drop me on a long article ▸ "Summarize"
  • Right-click ▸ Give Kiri a command ▸ "Turn these meeting notes into minutes"

About permissions

Nothing is needed right now. Your system asks only when you first use a feature:

  • Screen recording — only when you ask me about your screen. Screenshots stay in memory.
  • Microphone — only for voice questions.
  • Accessibility — only if you turn on hold-to-talk.
